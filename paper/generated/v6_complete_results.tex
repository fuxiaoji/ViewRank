\subsection{Complete retained-state experiment}\label{sec:v6complete}
The full-corpus retained-state matrix complements the preceding
prefix-corpus experiment. It evaluates 1,000 Hotpot and all 2,417 MuSiQue
questions on the larger graphs described above. All 46 configurations
complete three cold-state traces, retaining every source, strength and cutoff.
The default uses $L=128$, atom tolerance $10^{-3}$, and common output-driven
base and completion controls. Times cover the declared operator stages;
offline corpus construction remains separate.

At 4 MiB, exact interaction retention reduces complete operator time by
35.8\%/41.1\% against matched NoCoreResidual on Hotpot/MuSiQue, respectively.
Conditional completion saves 42.2\%/40.5\% against AlwaysFused.
Residual-budgeted atom preparation saves 55.1\%/16.6\% against fine atoms,
while output-driven base preparation saves 30.8\%/29.2\% against a fine base.
These comparisons isolate multiple substantial gains in complete-task
execution. Full-coordinate scans cost 40.690/24.802 s versus
42.326/26.750 s, so sparse candidate execution does not independently
improve complete time in this matrix.

Table~\ref{tab:v6complete} compares the default with the strongest complete-trace
baseline at every budget. The baseline minimizes the three-trace median over
NativeGap, OffsetGap, OSPGap, and both matched-budget DenseAtomic precisions.
Hotpot at 4 MiB saves 14.2\% in total time and lowers all-question p95 from
72.762 to 61.981 ms. At 16 MiB, total reductions are 3.5\% and 0.7\%.
MuSiQue's 1/4 MiB totals favor DenseAtomic; tails and observed process peaks
also remain mixed. These results support the component gains without
establishing uniform overall superiority.

Independent evaluation checks 2,829,276 explicit top-$k$ sets across all
138 traces, finding no missing, invalid or unresolved outputs. All 84 literal
differences from cold power-iteration references satisfy the fixed $10^{-12}$
boundary contract. The 6,555 cuts without strict separation retain their
numerical status. Their numerical-route views have persisted global
score-error bounds at most $5\times10^{-13}$, consistent with that membership
contract. These checks validate returned memberships and certificate flags;
the reported coordinates remain approximate scores.
